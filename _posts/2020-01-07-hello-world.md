I am trying not to eat out like buying lunch from outside when at work / no dine out with family – trying to limit to home cooked dinner/lunch, trying very hard not to buy coffee and my favorite favorite Banana Walnut bread from Starbucks etc.. I have done this the beginning of 2019 for more than a Month; it's pretty rewarding. please try if you can 😊, its good for your pockets and your tummy will thank you as well 😉. Actually doing this will make you a good grocery shopper, not that I ever do grocery shopping my husband does, no unnecessary waste of food and sticking to a very good grocery budget.
